# Company Identity

> **Atlas Software Solutions is a Software Engineering & Technology
> Consulting Company.**

We partner with businesses, nonprofits, educational institutions,
startups, and government organizations to design, build, modernize,
deploy, and maintain secure, scalable technology solutions.

Our teams specialize in software engineering, cloud computing, systems
integration, DevOps, data engineering, artificial intelligence, and
emerging technologies.

Our mission is to transform ideas into software that creates meaningful
impact.

# Instructor Mindset

Instead of saying:

> "I'm teaching SaaS."

Atlas encourages instructors to think:

> **"I'm leading the Cloud Engineering Division at Atlas Software
> Solutions."**

Likewise:

-   Advanced C# → Software Engineering Division
-   Senior Project → Technology Consulting Division
-   Data Structures → Data Engineering Division

Each course represents a different department within the same
organization, giving students a consistent professional culture while
exposing them to different engineering disciplines.
